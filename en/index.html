About

What is the Rhyme Game?

The Rhyme Game is a free freestyle rap trainer. A beat plays in the background while you improvise — on blank tiles you make up your own words, on colored ones you say the word shown. Every two lines the words rhyme, so you can practice smoothly linking improvisation with rhyme.

How do you practice freestyle rap?

Regular practice is the key. A new beat every day, new rhymes every day — just 5–10 minutes a day builds your sense of rhythm and word reflexes. You don't need any experience: the game guides you through every bar, and the difficulty grows with you.

Who is this game for?

For anyone who wants to improve their freestyle — from complete beginners to experienced MCs looking for daily practice. The game runs smoothly on phone and desktop, no install needed.

How to play?

1
Hit START — the beat starts playing, the cursor moves through the tiles on beat.
2
On gray tiles improvise — say whatever comes to mind.
3
On colored tiles say the shown word out loud, on beat.
4
Every two lines — the words rhyme. Try to smoothly blend improvisation with rhyme!
